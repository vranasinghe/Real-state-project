# Aura Space interface system

The user's latest preference restores the original Aura Space palette and homepage hero. Use bright emerald actions, white backgrounds and neutral-gray content surfaces. The homepage uses the original full-width mansion photo, forest-green overlay, white headline, floating search form and navigation that is transparent over the hero and white after scrolling. The estimator is a focused four-step workspace; the report reads as a technical case study.

- Headings: Plus Jakarta Sans; body: Inter, 16px base. Headings use tight tracking and balanced wrapping.
- Colors: emerald #27AE60 as brand accent; forest #1A3C34 for dark sections and the hero overlay; muted text #6B7280; white background #FFFFFF; light surfaces #F9FAFB; borders #E5E7EB. Emerald buttons use dark green text for accessible contrast; the original emerald remains #27AE60.
- Layout: 1280px maximum width, 24–32px gutters; 80px desktop section rhythm, 48px mobile. Cards use 16px radii and fine borders. Form controls are at least 44px tall.
- Keyboard focus is visible. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences. Photography uses consistent aspect ratios and lazy loading below the fold.
- Prediction results use tabular USD numerals. Error bands and global importance always carry interpretation and limitations. No confidence score or unrelated property valuation.
